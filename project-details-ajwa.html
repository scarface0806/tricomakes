Project Overview

Complete branding and promotional design system created for AJWA Ice Cream including banners, menu cards, flex designs, and print-ready marketing materials.

This project focused on building visually engaging promotional and branding materials for AJWA Ice Cream. The work included menu card design, flex banner creation, advertisement posters, and print-ready marketing creatives designed to attract customers and strengthen brand visibility.

Background

AJWA Ice Cream needed a strong, visually appealing set of marketing and promotional materials to attract customers, communicate product offerings, and build consistent brand visibility across their outlet and surrounding areas.

Trico Makes developed a complete print design system — from menu cards customers read in-store to large flex banners visible from the street — ensuring every touchpoint reinforced the brand's fresh, vibrant identity.

Design Scope

The design system needed to work across multiple formats — from small A5 menu cards to large outdoor flex banners — while maintaining visual consistency, vibrant energy, and product-focused storytelling that drives purchase decisions.

The design system uses vibrant colors, modern typography, and product-focused visual storytelling to create a strong and memorable customer experience at every interaction point.

Services delivered

  • Menu Card Design
  • Flex Banner Design
  • Poster & Advertisement Design
  • Print Branding Materials
  • Product Promotion Creatives
  • Marketing Visual Design

The Solution

Trico Makes delivered a cohesive suite of print and promotional designs that gave AJWA Ice Cream a strong, consistent visual identity across all customer touchpoints. Each piece was crafted to be print-ready, visually striking, and aligned with the brand's energy.

The completed materials — from in-store menu cards to large outdoor flex banners — now give AJWA Ice Cream a professional, eye-catching brand presence that attracts footfall and reinforces customer trust.
