Project Overview

Creative promotional posters designed for Jole Beauty Salon to improve local marketing visibility, customer engagement, service promotion, and branding presentation through visually attractive campaign creatives.

The campaign visuals were crafted for both print and digital usage, balancing premium beauty aesthetics with offer-led communication for stronger conversion impact.

Background

The salon needed campaign creatives that looked premium while still clearly communicating service offers and local promotions. Existing marketing visuals lacked consistency and visual hierarchy.

Trico Makes delivered a campaign poster set with strong brand tone, attractive composition, and conversion-oriented messaging suitable for social media and local advertisements.

The Challenges

The key challenge was creating high-engagement beauty campaign visuals that feel luxurious yet remain readable for promotional messaging and seasonal offers.

The design system needed to work across multiple formats including social media posts, local business promotions, and print-ready collateral while preserving brand consistency.

Key campaign features

  • Promotional Poster Design
  • Beauty Salon Branding
  • Marketing Campaign Creatives
  • Offer-Based Design Layouts
  • Social Media Poster Design
  • Local Business Advertisement Creatives
  • High-Engagement Visual Design

The Solution

Trico Makes created a polished poster campaign direction for Jole Beauty Salon with distinct visual themes, clear promotional hierarchy, and stronger brand recall.

The final creatives are optimized for customer attraction, engagement, and local business visibility across both online and offline campaign touchpoints.
