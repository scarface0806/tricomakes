Project Overview

TapVyo is a smart digital business card platform developed with NFC technology to simplify networking and business identity sharing. Users can instantly share contact information, social links, websites, and professional details with a single tap.

The platform focuses on modern UI/UX, responsive design, fast performance, and seamless accessibility across devices � making professional networking effortless in a digital-first world.

Background

Traditional paper business cards are easily lost, outdated, and environmentally costly. TapVyo was conceived to replace them with a smarter, always-up-to-date digital alternative that works with a single NFC tap or QR code scan.

Trico Makes designed and developed the full platform � from the user-facing profile interface to the admin dashboard � creating an experience that feels instant, intuitive, and professional for both the card holder and the recipient.

The Challenges

The core challenge was building a platform that felt completely frictionless � the shared profile needed to load instantly, display beautifully on any device, and work without the recipient needing an app or account.

Balancing feature richness with simplicity was equally critical. The platform needed to support multiple link types, custom branding, and real-time profile updates without overwhelming the user interface.

Key priorities for the platform

  • NFC-Based Smart Contact Sharing
  • Digital Profile Management
  • Social Media Integration
  • Mobile Responsive Interface
  • Fast & Secure Performance
  • Modern UI/UX Experience

The Solution

Trico Makes built TapVyo as a fully responsive web platform where users create a shareable digital profile linked to their NFC card or QR code. Each profile supports contact details, social links, website URLs, and custom branding � all editable in real time.

The result is a polished, high-performance platform that makes sharing professional identity as simple as a tap. TapVyo delivers a seamless networking experience that works on every device, without friction or technical barriers.
