Project Overview

Currently redesigning the official Kaliammal College website with a modern, responsive, and user-friendly interface. The project focuses on improving user experience, navigation structure, mobile responsiveness, performance optimization, and overall visual design to create a more engaging digital presence for students and visitors.

Trico Makes is leading the full redesign effort ? from UX planning and wireframing to front-end development ? with the goal of transforming the college's online presence into a student-friendly, fast, and visually compelling platform.

Background

Kaliammal College's existing website did not reflect the institution's quality and reputation. The outdated layout, poor mobile experience, and slow performance were creating friction for prospective students, parents, and faculty looking for information.

Trico Makes was engaged to reimagine the website from the ground up ? building a cleaner, faster, and more intuitive digital experience that better serves the college community and supports student recruitment.

The Challenges

The key challenge was redesigning a complex institutional website that serves multiple audiences ? students, parents, faculty, and administrators ? while keeping the navigation simple and the content well-organised.

The redesign also needed to prioritise mobile responsiveness and page speed, as a large portion of visitors access the site on mobile devices. Modernising the visual identity without losing institutional familiarity was an additional consideration.

Key priorities for the redesign

  • Modern UI/UX improvements across all pages
  • Fully responsive design for all screen sizes
  • Optimized performance and fast loading
  • Better navigation experience for students and visitors
  • Updated visual identity aligned with the college brand
  • Student-friendly interface and content structure

The Solution

Trico Makes is redesigning the full Kaliammal College website using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Bootstrap. The new layout is built around a student-first experience ? with clear course listings, intuitive navigation, and a clean visual hierarchy that makes finding information effortless.

The redesigned website will deliver a professional, modern, and fully responsive experience that strengthens the college's digital credibility, improves engagement with prospective students, and supports the institution's long-term growth goals.
