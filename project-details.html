Project Overview

A modern textile business website designed and developed for Real Textiles. The website showcases textile products, company information, product categories, and enquiry features with a clean and responsive UI.

Trico Makes built the complete website from the ground up ? focusing on a fast-loading, mobile-friendly experience that helps Real Textiles present their product range clearly and capture customer enquiries effectively.

Background

Real Textiles needed a professional online presence that could clearly communicate their product offerings to buyers and business clients. The goal was to create a website that reflects the quality of their textile products while making it easy for visitors to browse categories and get in touch.

Trico Makes planned the site architecture around product discovery and enquiry conversion ? ensuring the layout, typography, and imagery all reinforce the brand's credibility in the textile industry.

The Challenges

The primary challenge was designing a website that could showcase a diverse range of textile products in an organised, visually appealing way while keeping the user experience simple and intuitive.

The site also needed to work seamlessly across all devices and load quickly, as buyers often browse on mobile. Building trust through clean design and clear product information was equally important.

Key priorities for the project

  • Responsive design across all screen sizes
  • Clear product category sections for easy navigation
  • Fast loading performance for better user experience
  • Functional contact and enquiry form
  • Modern UI/UX that reflects the brand's quality

The Solution

Trico Makes designed and developed the full website for Real Textiles using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Bootstrap. The layout was structured around intuitive product category browsing, with dedicated sections for company information and a streamlined enquiry form.

The final website delivers a clean, professional presentation of Real Textiles' product range with fast load times, full mobile responsiveness, and a modern UI that builds confidence with prospective buyers and business clients.
