Project Overview

A modern textile industry website designed to showcase products, improve brand presence, and provide a seamless user experience across devices. Trico Makes designed and developed the complete Somakannan Textiles website, focusing on clean product presentation, easy navigation, and a responsive layout that works beautifully on both desktop and mobile.

The project centred on building a credible digital identity for Somakannan Textiles � helping the business effectively showcase its textile collections to buyers and customers while creating a strong, professional brand presence online.

Background

Somakannan Textiles is an established textile business with a rich product range covering fabrics, collections, and textile categories. Despite their strong offline presence, the business lacked a modern digital platform to effectively showcase their products and reach customers online.

Trico Makes was engaged to build a complete, brand-focused website that would bring the business's textile offerings to the digital space � helping them attract new customers, present their collection professionally, and establish a credible online identity in the textile industry.

The Challenges

The primary challenge was designing a website that could present a diverse textile product range in an organised and visually appealing way, while keeping the user experience clean and intuitive for buyers browsing on both desktop and mobile devices.

Ensuring the site loaded fast, communicated product quality through design, and encouraged customer enquiries required a careful balance of visual design, content structure, and performance optimisation.

Key priorities for the project

  • Responsive design across all screen sizes and devices
  • Organised product category sections for easy browsing
  • Fast-loading performance for improved user experience
  • Modern UI/UX that reflects the brand's quality and identity
  • Brand-focused visual presentation of textile collections
  • Easy navigation structure for first-time visitors

Features

The Somakannan Textiles website was built with a comprehensive feature set designed to deliver a premium browsing experience and drive customer engagement across all touchpoints.

The Solution

Trico Makes designed and developed the complete Somakannan Textiles website with a focus on clean product presentation and a modern, brand-aligned visual identity. The website was built using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Bootstrap � structured around intuitive product category browsing, with dedicated sections for collections and a seamless enquiry experience.

The final website delivers a clean, professional digital presence for Somakannan Textiles � with fast load times, full mobile responsiveness, and a modern UI that builds trust with prospective buyers and business clients, while effectively showcasing the breadth of their textile offerings.
