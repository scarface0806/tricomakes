Project Overview

This branding project includes the creation of a modern technology-focused corporate logo for AppnStruct and a vibrant product branding identity for Oranzone. Both brands are part of the same company ecosystem.

The designs were created with a strong focus on scalability, recognition, modern visual language, and cross-platform usability to establish a professional and memorable brand presence for both identities.

Background

AppnStruct required a strong, professional corporate identity that could communicate their expertise as an IT company clearly and instantly — both digitally and in print. Meanwhile, their product brand Oranzone needed a distinct, energetic identity that could stand independently in the market.

Trico Makes approached both identities as a unified branding system, ensuring visual coherence between the parent company and its product line while keeping each brand's character unique and recognisable.

Branding Scope

The project demanded two distinct visual directions — a minimal, black-and-white tech identity for AppnStruct, and a vibrant, symbol-driven identity for Oranzone — while ensuring both logos could scale across digital platforms, print materials, and merchandise.

The Solution

For AppnStruct, Trico Makes crafted a bold black-and-white wordmark with a geometric symbol that conveys structure, precision, and technological authority — designed to scale perfectly from business cards to digital banners.

For Oranzone, the team developed a vibrant logo combining warm, energetic tones with a clean symbolic mark that communicates product personality and brand confidence. Both identities now form a cohesive branding ecosystem that works across all platforms and touchpoints.
