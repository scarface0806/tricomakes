Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know before making a decision.

Are there any hidden charges beyond the listed prices?
Absolutely not. The price you see is what you pay. Any third-party costs like domain registration, hosting, or stock images are communicated upfront before the project begins — never as a surprise at the end.
What is your refund policy?
We require a 50% advance to start the project. Once work begins, the advance is non-refundable. However, if you are unsatisfied with the initial concepts, we work with you through revision rounds to make it right before the final delivery and balance payment.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?
Yes. For monthly retainer services (SEO, Social Media), you can upgrade or downgrade at the end of any billing cycle. For one-time projects like website design or branding, you can always purchase an upgrade add-on or move to a higher tier on your next project.
How do I communicate with the team during the project?
All projects are managed via WhatsApp for quick communication and email for formal approvals and deliverables. You get a dedicated point of contact throughout the entire project lifecycle — no support tickets, no bots.
Do you offer custom or enterprise pricing?
Yes. For large-scale projects, multi-service bundles, or enterprise-level requirements, we offer custom pricing. Book a free consultation and we'll scope out exactly what you need and provide a tailored quote — no obligation.
How long does it take to see SEO results?
SEO is a long-term strategy. Most clients start seeing meaningful keyword movement in 2–3 months, with significant traffic growth typically visible at the 4–6 month mark. We track and report progress bi-weekly so you are never in the dark about how your investment is performing.
Is there a lock-in contract for monthly services?
No long-term lock-in. We recommend a minimum 3-month commitment for SEO and Social Media to see results, but you can cancel at the end of any month with 15 days' notice. We earn your continued business through results, not contracts.
