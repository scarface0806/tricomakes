Pricing Questions Answered

Are these prices fixed or negotiable? ▼
These are starting prices. Final cost depends on scope, complexity, and timeline. Contact us for a custom quote — we always find something that works for your budget.
Do you offer EMI or installment payment? ▼
Yes. For projects above ₹15,000 we offer a simple 50% advance and 50% on delivery structure. For larger projects, milestone-based payments can be arranged.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept UPI, bank transfer, and all major payment apps. We provide a proper invoice for every project.
Is there a refund policy? ▼
We do not offer refunds after work has begun, but we offer revision rounds on every project to make sure you are fully satisfied with the output.
Can I start with a small package and upgrade later? ▼
Absolutely. Many clients start with a Basic website or Logo Only package and expand to full branding or marketing retainers as their business grows.
